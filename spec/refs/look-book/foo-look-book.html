2 · Colours

Every colour, light on top, dark below

Each square shows the light value (top half) and the dark value (bottom half). Indigo is the one brand colour. Amber, green and red only ever mean "needs you", "done" and "failed", never decoration. All text pairs pass the readability rule (WCAG AA).

3 · Type

One font, twelve sizes

Segoe UI Variable for everything, the font built into Windows. Hindi and Punjabi switch to Nirmala UI on their own. Nothing is ever smaller than 12px, and message text is always 15px.

5 · Icons

77 icons, all from one set

Microsoft's Fluent icons (thin "Regular" style, free to use). I checked every one of the 77 names in the spec against the real set: all 77 exist, so builders won't have to substitute anything. Shown at their real 20px size.

6 · The living face

"Two eyes", the moods you'll see most

Drawn from the exact eye sizes and positions in the spec (eyes 12% wide, 24% tall, at 36% and 64%). The face only changes when something real happens: a job running, a card waiting, a job done with proof. Helpers wear the same eyes on their own colour.

7 · Logo · three drafts, pick one (question 1)

The logo and the wordmark

The spec gives the logo's rules (a disc with a heavy lowercase "f", 56% of the height, nudged 2% right, crossbar hanging left) but no drawing, so each builder would draw their own. When I drew it exactly to those rules, it came out looking a lot like Facebook's "f". So here are three drafts; question 1 asks you to pick one.
